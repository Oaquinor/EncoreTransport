Questions before you travel

A few things you may want to know.

Quick answers for planning a trip, choosing seats and tracking a package.

How do I find an Encore Transport trip?

Choose your departure city, destination, travel date and number of passengers. We’ll show the departures currently available for that search.

Can I choose my seat before I travel?

Yes. After choosing a departure, continue to Passenger to see the seats currently available for that bus and select the seats you want.

Can I see the route before booking?

Yes. When route information is available, you can review the trip map and estimated travel time before continuing.

How do I track a package?

Open Package Tracking and enter the tracking number provided for your shipment to see its latest recorded status.
