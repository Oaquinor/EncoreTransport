FAQ

Quick answers before booking.

Can I book from the website?

Yes. The website sends passengers into the Encore Passenger app to search, choose seats, review, and see a digital ticket.

Are payments live in this preview?

No. Payment states are mocked so the experience can be demonstrated without charging real cards.

Does the preview use real tracking?

No. Maps and tracking are represented with preview-ready placeholders that show where real integrations will connect later.
